## What the number means

`first_trade_px − copula_price` is the room a quoter had. It is **negative**: combos win at
prices ~2.5¢ *below* our correlation-adjusted fair value. Incumbent quoters are pricing at or
inside our model's fair, so there is no gap to stand in — undercutting the winning quote means
quoting below fair. Whatever share of the historical +3.17¢/ct seller edge is real, it is
captured at prices our model says are already too low; a new entrant with this model cannot join
the flow at positive expectancy. (Either incumbents are sharper than the fitted copula or the
copula overprices the joint — for the gate it does not matter which.)

Positive pockets, reported for completeness and **not** gate-clearing: `same_player`
(n = 693, median −0.29¢, mean +1.65¢, 47% > 0 — small, mean dragged by a right tail) and 2-leg
(32% > 0, mean +0.49¢, median still −1.47¢). Neither crosses the pre-registered line on the
median, and per the handoff: STOP is reported plainly, not explained away.
